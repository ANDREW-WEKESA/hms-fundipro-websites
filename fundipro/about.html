About FundiPro

Empowering communities through skilled craftsmanship

🎯

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between skilled professionals and customers seeking quality services by providing a trusted, transparent, and efficient platform that empowers artisans and delivers excellence to every household and business.

🌟

Our Vision

To become the leading platform for skilled services across Africa, creating opportunities for millions of artisans while ensuring every customer has access to reliable, professional services at their fingertips.

Our Story

Building connections since 2020

2020

The Beginning

FundiPro was founded with a simple idea: make it easier for people to find trusted skilled professionals in their community. We started with just 50 fundis in Nairobi.

2021

Rapid Growth

Our platform expanded to 5 cities across Kenya, connecting over 2,000 professionals with thousands of satisfied customers. We introduced our rating and review system.

2022

Innovation

Launched our mobile app and introduced instant booking features. We also started our training program to help fundis enhance their skills and business practices.

2023

Regional Expansion

Extended our services to neighboring countries, reaching over 10,000 professionals and completing more than 50,000 jobs with a 4.8-star average rating.

2024

Today

FundiPro continues to grow, serving communities across East Africa with cutting-edge technology and an unwavering commitment to quality and trust.

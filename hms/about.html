About HMS

Three decades of excellence in healthcare delivery

Our Story

Building healthier communities since 2005

Founded in 2005 in Nairobi, HMS began as a small community clinic with a clear purpose: to make quality, affordable healthcare accessible to every Kenyan. Over two decades that clinic has grown into a leading multi-specialty hospital serving patients across East Africa.

Today HMS operates with 200 beds, 50+ specialist doctors, and a full suite of clinical departments — all underpinned by the values of compassion, excellence, integrity, and innovation that have guided us from the very beginning.

Beyond clinical care, we remain deeply committed to the communities we serve through free medical camps, health education initiatives, and financial assistance programmes that ensure no patient is turned away.

Our Journey

Two decades of growth and healthcare excellence

2005

Founded

Founded as a small clinic in Nairobi, serving the local community with primary healthcare services.

2008

Expanded to Full Hospital

Expanded to a full-service hospital with 50 beds, offering inpatient and outpatient care across multiple departments.

2012

KMPDC Full Accreditation

Received full accreditation from the Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council, confirming our compliance with national healthcare standards.

2015

ICU & Maternity Wing Opened

Opened a dedicated Intensive Care Unit and a purpose-built Maternity Wing, significantly expanding our specialist care capabilities.

2018

ISO 9001:2015 Certification

Achieved ISO 9001:2015 certification for our quality management systems, reinforcing our commitment to continuous improvement.

2020

COVID-19 Designated Isolation Centre

Designated as an official COVID-19 isolation and treatment centre by the Ministry of Health, playing a critical role in Kenya's pandemic response.

2023

Major Expansion

Expanded to 200 beds and recruited 50+ specialist doctors, making HMS one of the largest community hospitals in the region.

2025

Digital Health Transformation

Launched the HMS Patient Portal, enabling digital appointment booking, online lab results, and remote consultations as part of our digital health transformation.

About HMS

Three decades of excellence in healthcare delivery

Our Story

Building healthier communities since 1994

Founded in 1994, Hospital Management System (HMS) began with a simple yet powerful vision: to provide world-class healthcare that is accessible, affordable, and compassionate. What started as a 100-bed facility has grown into one of the region's most trusted multi-specialty hospitals.

Today, HMS stands as a beacon of medical excellence with over 1000 beds, 500+ doctors, and 50+ specialized departments. Our state-of-the-art infrastructure combines advanced medical technology with a patient-centered approach, ensuring that every individual receives personalized care tailored to their unique needs.

We have pioneered numerous medical breakthroughs in the region, from performing the first successful robotic surgery to establishing comprehensive cancer care protocols. Our commitment to continuous innovation and medical education keeps us at the forefront of healthcare delivery.

Beyond clinical excellence, we believe in giving back to our community through health awareness programs, free medical camps, and preventive care initiatives. At HMS, we don't just treat illnesses—we partner with you on your journey to optimal health and wellbeing.

What to Expect

Your appointment journey in three simple steps

📅

1. Book Your Appointment

Complete the online booking form and select your preferred date, time, and doctor. You'll receive instant confirmation via email and SMS.

✅

2. Confirmation & Reminders

Our team will confirm your appointment and send you reminders. Feel free to call us if you need to reschedule or have questions.

🏥

3. Visit & Consultation

Arrive 15 minutes early for registration. Our staff will guide you through check-in, and you'll be seen by your doctor at the scheduled time.

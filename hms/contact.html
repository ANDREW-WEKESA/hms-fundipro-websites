Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

You can book an appointment through our Appointments page, by calling Reception on +254 20 123 4567 (Mon–Fri 8 AM–5 PM), or by visiting our reception desk in person. For urgent consultations outside office hours, please use the Emergency line.

We accept SHA (Social Health Authority), NHIF (for existing members), AAR Insurance, Jubilee Insurance, Britam, CIC Insurance, Madison Insurance, Resolution Health, and most other private insurers. We also accept M-Pesa and cash. Contact Billing & Insurance on Ext. 550 for specific queries.

We are located on Ngong Road, Upper Hill, Nairobi, Kenya — near Kenyatta National Hospital. Matatu routes 23 and 125 stop nearby. Paid on-site parking and a wheelchair-accessible ambulance bay are available. See the map below for directions.

General visiting hours are Mon–Fri 8 AM–5 PM and Saturday 8 AM–1 PM. On Sundays and public holidays only the Emergency Department is open. ICU and maternity ward visits may have additional restrictions — please call the relevant department before arriving.

Lab results are typically ready within 24–48 hours for routine tests. You can collect them in person at the Laboratory (Ext. 530), or access them through your Patient Portal. Urgent results are communicated directly to your doctor, who will contact you as soon as they are available.

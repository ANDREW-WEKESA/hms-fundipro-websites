Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment online through our appointments page, call our appointment line at +254 20 700 1234, or visit our reception desk in person.

What documents should I bring?

Please bring a valid photo ID, insurance card (if applicable), list of current medications, and any previous medical records relevant to your visit.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes, we accept walk-ins for OPD services, but appointments are recommended to minimize waiting time. Emergency services are always available without appointment.

What insurance do you accept?

We accept SHA (Social Health Authority), NHIF (for existing members), AAR Insurance, Jubilee Insurance, Britam, CIC Insurance, Madison Insurance, Resolution Health, and other private insurers. We also accept M-Pesa and cash payments. Contact our Billing & Insurance department at +254 20 700 1270 for specific inquiries.

How the Portal Works

Getting started is simple

📝

1. Register

Create your account using your email and patient information. You'll need your patient ID or date of birth to verify your identity. Registration takes less than 5 minutes.

🔐

2. Login Securely

Access the portal anytime using your email and password. Two-factor authentication provides additional security for your sensitive health information.

🏥

3. Manage Your Health

View records, book appointments, access test results, request prescriptions, pay bills, and communicate with your healthcare team - all in one place.

Your Privacy & Security

HIPAA compliant and secure

🔒

Bank-Level Security

Your health information is protected with 256-bit encryption, the same security standard used by financial institutions. All data transmission is encrypted and secure.

🛡️

HIPAA Compliant

We fully comply with HIPAA regulations to ensure your protected health information (PHI) remains confidential and is only accessed by authorized healthcare professionals.

✅

Two-Factor Authentication

Optional two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security to your account, requiring both your password and a verification code to login.

👁️

Access Control

You control who can access your health information. Grant or revoke access to family members or caregivers at any time through your account settings.

🔐 Your Privacy Matters: We never sell or share your personal health information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is used solely for your healthcare and is protected by strict privacy policies and federal law.

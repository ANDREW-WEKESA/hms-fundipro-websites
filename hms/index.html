How the Claims Process Works

1

Verify

Present your insurance card at reception. We verify your cover and benefits in real time before treatment begins.

2

Pre-auth

Our billing team requests pre-authorisation from your insurer for procedures, admissions, or high-cost treatments.

3

Claim

We submit the claim directly to your insurer on your behalf. You only pay any co-payment or uncovered balance.
